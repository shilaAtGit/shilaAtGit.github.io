MetBooks — 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

中文

版本：1.0
生效日期：2026-10-12

本地处理与保存

MetBooks 用于在本地导入和阅读用户选择的 EPUB 与 PDF 文件。书籍内容、书籍名称、本地用户昵称、阅读权限配置和阅读位置保存在 App 的本地存储中。

口令与安全

本地用户口令与阅读权限管理口令用于保护对应操作。App不保存明文口令；验证信息保存在系统安全区域中。阅读权限是本 App 内的限制，不是系统级家长控制。

网络与数据收集

App 不主动访问互联网，不向开发者服务器上传书籍或用户信息，也不包含广告、跟踪或使用分析服务。

导入、备份与系统服务

导入、备份和恢复通过系统文件选择器进行。书籍备份包含书籍内容与书籍元数据，不包含本地用户、口令、阅读权限或个人阅读位置。你选择的位置可能属于云盘或其他文件提供商；这些服务以及系统设备备份由你和系统设置控制，并适用各自的隐私政策。

删除与保留

你可以在 App 内删除书籍，并使用本地用户自己的口令删除该用户及其阅读权限和阅读位置。系统“卸载 App”保留 App 数据；系统“删除 App”移除 App 的本地数据。MetBooks 会在删除后的全新安装首次启动时清理旧验证信息。你另存的书籍备份需自行删除。

政策更新与联系

此离线政策随 App 版本更新。如对本地数据处理有疑问，请使用下方支持邮箱联系

English

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-12

Local processing and storage

MetBooks imports and reads EPUB and PDF files selected by you. Book content, book titles, local reader nicknames, reading permissions, and reading positions are stored locally in the app.

Passcodes and security

Local reader passwords and the reading permissions passcode protect the relevant actions. The app does not store plaintext passwords. Password verification information is stored securely by the system. Reading permissions apply within this app and are not system parental controls.

Network access and data collection

The app does not access the internet itself or upload books or reader information to developer servers. It contains no advertising, tracking, or usage analytics services.

Import, backup, and system services

Import, backup, and restore use the system file picker. Book backups contain book content and metadata, but exclude local readers, passwords, reading permissions, and personal reading positions. Your chosen location may belong to a cloud drive or another file provider. Those services and system device backups are controlled by you and system settings and have their own privacy policies.

Deletion and retention

You can delete books in the app and use a local reader’s own password to delete that reader and their permissions and reading positions. Using “Offload App” preserves app data; using “Delete App” removes local app data. MetBooks clears old verification information on the first launch of a fresh installation after deletion. Separately saved book backups must be deleted by you.

Policy updates and contact

This offline policy is updated with app releases. For questions about local data handling, contact the support email below.
