User Guide for the App.
Continuously being improved.

Registration and Login

Registration is entirely local—no information is sent to the server. I define who I am.

Logging in simply notifies your friends that you are online. No other information is transmitted to the server.

Note 1: You can register multiple times locally. Each registration creates a brand new identity. You can reduce the workload of re-registering by backing up and restoring your groups and contacts.

Note 2: The login password is also stored entirely locally. If you forget your password, it cannot be recovered through customer support or conventional methods. You will need to re-register a new local user.

Adding Contacts

This app is designed as a chat tool for real-life friends. Contacts can only be added in person, face to face. No online friend addition, no random chat, and no anonymous chat are supported.

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, then choose “Friend Passphrase”.
  2. In the pop-up “My Private QR Code” screen, select a channel and generate the QR code.

Note 1: Contacts are organized by channel. A channel is essentially one of your friend “circles.” A good practice is to make each channel correspond to a real-life group of people who all know each other.

Note 2: Adding contacts does not require any network connection.

  1. Be scanned. After your friend scans your QR code, they will obtain your channel information and your profile.

  2. Scan back. After your friend also generates their QR code, you scan your friend’s QR code.

Note 1: Your friend needs to show their QR code and select the same channel that you chose when generating your QR code.

Note 2: You need to go to the “Contacts” tab, tap the camera icon or the "+" icon, grant camera permissions, and use the camera to scan the QR code. After scanning back, you can start chatting.

  1. Adding more contacts. You must complete the full be-scanned + scan-back process before you can proceed to add the next contact.

Backup / Restore Groups and Contacts

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, choose “Backup Groups and Contacts”, enter a passphrase, select a backup location (local storage is recommended), and tap “Move” / “Save” to complete the backup.

Note 1: This feature is designed for restoring groups and contacts after reinstalling the app or re-registering an account. However, for security reasons, only the most basic information is backed up.

Note 2: Only the following are backed up: basic contact information, basic channel information, and the associations between contacts and channels.

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, choose “Restore Groups and Contacts”, enter the passphrase, and select the backup file to restore. The restoration will proceed automatically, and the restored contacts and channels will be displayed.

Note 1: Restoration requires the passphrase used to encrypt the backup file.

Note 2: After restoration, contacts retain only their names. This is intended to make it easier for you to meet your friends in person and re-add them via the full be-scanned + scan-back process.

Note 3: Since no other data is backed up, all messages from all contacts are lost, and all friends must be re-added through the complete face-to-face QR code process.

Offline / Online

  1. After logging in, select the “Chats” tab, expand any channel, and wait for the channel green light to turn on. You are now online.

  2. Collapse the channel. When all channels are collapsed, you are offline.

Note 1: For two people to chat in real time, both must be online in the same channel simultaneously.

Note 2: You can send messages to offline friends, but they will receive them with a delay. In the worst case, they may not receive the messages until the next time both of you are online at the same time.

Enhanced Security

Enabling Apple Attest security services can improve the security level of the chat service, primarily to prevent malicious attacks on the server.

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, and tap “Enhanced Security” to toggle this feature on or off.

Show / Hide Messages

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, choose “App Settings”, and tap “Show Messages” to toggle message display on or off.

Hidden messages are displayed as ****.

Pixel Style

The app also offers a pixel style, which fits a more minimalist aesthetic.

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, choose “App Settings”, and tap “Pixel Style” to switch between pixel style and the normal style.

Block a Contact

On the Contact Details page, you can block a contact.

Messages from blocked contacts will not be visible, and you will not receive new message notifications from them.

You can still see the online status of blocked contacts.

Report / Complain

On the Contact Details page, you can file a complaint or report against a contact.

Configure Your Own Server Address

If you have set up your own server, you can switch to using it for your chat services.

  1. After logging in, select the “Me” tab, choose “App Settings”, and enter the server’s public IP address and the app’s port number (default is 12345) under “Server Configuration”.

  2. Tap “Save Configuration”, and select “Restart Now” when prompted.

  3. After logging back in, the new server address will take effect. You are now using your own dedicated server.
