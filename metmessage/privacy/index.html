Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how MetMessage (hereinafter referred to as “this App”) collects, uses, and protects your information. This App is developed and operated by shila and shila.com (hereinafter referred to as “we” or “us”).

Core Principle: This App does NOT collect, upload, or store any of your personal information on any server. All data is stored exclusively on your local device.

1. Information We Collect

This App does not collect any personal information.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location information, device identifiers (such as IDFA), or any other personal information.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools.
  • We do not track your device or behavior through this App or any third-party partners.

2. Data Storage and Location

  • All of your data (including but not limited to: user identity, contact list, chat history, App settings) is stored exclusively on your local device (within the App sandbox).
  • Your data is never uploaded to any cloud server.
  • The server for this App is only responsible for relaying real-time messages in transit. It does not record or permanently store any chat content or user information.

3. Camera Usage

This App requests access to your camera only in the following scenario:

  • Scanning QR codes to add friends: When you use the “Scan” feature, the App accesses the camera to scan the QR code of another user.
  • Avatar Setting: When you use the “Set Avatar” feature, this App will access your photo library and albums.

4. Local Storage

This App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your user identity information
  • Your contact list
  • Your chat history

All of this data exists only on your device. You can completely erase this data by uninstalling the App or using the “Delete Account” feature within the App.

5. Email Sending

When you use the “Report” feature in this App, the App generates an encrypted log file containing the reported user’s information and the most recent 50 chat messages, and sends it to our support email address via the iOS system’s built-in mail composer (MFMailComposeViewController).

  • The email only contains: the reported user’s ID, the description of the complaint, and the most recent 50 chat messages. It does not include any of your personal information (such as your ID).
  • The encryption key is held solely by us, ensuring the security of the data during transmission.
  • The email is initiated by you; the App does not automatically send any emails on its own.

6. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all of your data through the following methods:

  1. Deletion within the App: Navigate to “Settings” → “Delete Account”, and confirm your choice. All of your local data will be permanently erased.
  2. Uninstall the App: Uninstalling this App will simultaneously remove all data stored on your device.

7. Children’s Privacy

This App is not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from users of any age (as we do not collect any information at all). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with any information, please contact us using the information below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
